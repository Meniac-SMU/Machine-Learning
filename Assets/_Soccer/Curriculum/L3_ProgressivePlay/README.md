# L3 Progressive Play

L3-A starts only after the unchanged strict L2 handoff: an explicit kick toward an advantageous teammate, at least 2 m of real ball displacement, and that designated teammate as the current confirmed carrier. Incidental contact cannot start the continuation.

The designated receiver must remain the last toucher and within 2.4 m of the ball for at least 0.35 seconds, then be the current confirmed carrier when it has advanced the ball at least 3 m toward the Navy goal within 6 seconds. This physical-retention clock starts only after the strict L2 confirmed handoff. Before stable retention, the receiver has at most 2 seconds from reception to chase a ball that rebounds outside 2.4 m; out-of-range time gives no stable-control or progress reward and resets the stable clock. After stable retention, excessive ball distance immediately fails. A different confirmed carrier or touch, any kick after reception, or timeout also ends the attempt without success. A temporary null carrier identity neither proves completion nor erases otherwise continuous physical control. The progress reward is `0.15` at most and follows the highest forward displacement measured from the reception point, so backward-forward movement and repeated touches cannot farm reward. Final completion remains group `0.4` plus receiver `0.1`.

The first bounded run uses 32 executable workers and initializes from the approved L2-capable `CurriculumL1-20260905-r018` checkpoint. `soccer_l2_pass_advice=1` keeps the already documented pass direction/support technical aid. Because the flight-only receiver chase ended before 0.35 seconds and produced about 3% stable receptions in r001-r003, L3 extends that same recorded chase only until stable retention is established. It then returns movement to the Neural policy; L3 adds no 3 m forward-motion controller. Fixed evaluation reports this intervention and its event frequency.

On the approved r018 policy with the same fixed seed and 100 episodes, the bounded recovery changed L3 success from 2% to 8%, stable receiver possession from 7% to 11%, mean forward progress from 0.19 m to 0.51 m, and distance failures from 89% to 27%. The 62% actual different-player touches during recovery remained failures. This opens a measurable signal for the bounded r004 training run without weakening the success contract.

The r004 fixed evaluation reached 14% success and 18% stable receiver possession, but 44% of episodes still failed on another player's physical touch before stable control and 30% failed on distance. A fixed comparison that stopped non-receiver Red teammates until stable control regressed success to 9% and stable possession to 16%, so that 97-event/90.88-second intervention was removed and is not part of the active curriculum. Reward review then found that forward-progress shaping was being paid before stable control. The active contract now starts both its forward high-water mark and progress payout only after the designated receiver has held stable physical control for 0.35 seconds. The reward cap and completion conditions remain unchanged.

The first 100k stable-control-first run reached 23% in the same-seed fixed evaluation, up from r004's 14%. Its last five training summaries ranged from 8.96% to 22.39% success and 14.93% to 31.34% stable possession. This is sufficient evidence for one same-optimizer resume to 200k; it is not promotion evidence, and no further simple extension is allowed if the formal 50%/85% window remains closed.

At 200k, the saved 149,876 and final 200,064 checkpoints reached 33% and 29% in the same fixed 100-episode evaluation, while stable possession remained 41% and 42%. Because roughly 70–80% of stable receptions already completed the advance, the next isolated reward change adds one receiver-only `0.1` milestone when the same designated last toucher first holds the ball within 2.4 m for 0.35 seconds. Null carrier gaps, out-of-range recovery, incidental touches, and repeated control cannot earn it. Existing progress and completion rewards remain unchanged.

Promotion requires five aligned summaries with L3 success at least 50%, initial possession at least 85%, strict pass completion at least 85%, stable receiver possession at least 85%, and episode time at most 15 seconds. A separate difficulty 1, waiting 0, pass gate 0 fixed 300-episode evaluation plus L0/L1/L2 regression evaluations are mandatory.
